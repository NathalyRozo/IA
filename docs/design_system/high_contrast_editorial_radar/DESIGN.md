## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an unapologetic, hyper-tactile, high-contrast editorial interface built for fast synthesis, cross-spectrum comparison, and deep perspective tracking. It moves past generic news aggregation by fusing energetic contemporary digital editorialism with the visual rigor of high-velocity financial terminals.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Incisive & Vigilant:** Pierces algorithmic clutter with high signal, razor-sharp visual hierarchies, and zero fluff.
- **Electric & Objective:** Electric yellow signals breaking focal points without synthetic alarmism. Data surfaces convey intellectual balance, multi-source accountability, and rigor.
- **Dynamic & Tactile:** Generously rounded structural primitives combined with punchy chromatic markers create a physical, inviting instrument for analysis.

### Design Style: Hyper-Editorial Neo-Brutalist Minimalism
The style marries minimalist, structured content architecture with high-voltage neon accents, dense deep obsidian foundations, and hyper-rounded card geometries (20px–24px). High-contrast surfaces let information pop instantly, while deliberate circular badges and multi-hue accent fills anchor diverse journalistic perspectives.

## Colors

The palette leverages a high-contrast dual-canvas paradigm, configured by default to dark mode for deep visual focus and radar-style reading.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#FFE600`):** Electric Yellow. The primary brand signal. Applied to focal action elements, breaking alerts, selected tabs, and primary action states. Always paired with Obsidian (`#0B0F19`) text to maintain strict WCAG AAA contrast.
- **Secondary (`#FF007F`):** Vibrant Magenta. Editorial bias indicators, sharp counter-perspectives, high-delta trending vectors, and critical divergence markers.
- **Tertiary (`#00D09C`):** Mint Cyan Green. Verifications, factual consensus markers, positive momentum metrics, and live telemetry badges.
- **Extended Accents:**
  - **Royal Blue (`#0066FF`):** Institutional sources, deep analytical readouts, policy tracking.
  - **Vibrant Orange (`#FF6B00`):** Developing stories, contextual alerts, opinion spectrums.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - **Dark Base Canvas (`#0B0F19`):** Deep Obsidian for total immersion.
  - **Dark Elevators (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Navy Slate tiers for grouped data sets and stacked structural modules.
  - **Light Mode Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft light neutral backdrop paired with pure `#FFFFFF` elevated panels.
  - **Border Strokes:** `#334155` at 50% opacity in dark mode; `#E2E8F0` in light mode.

### Contrast Discipline
Chromatic accents are never used for long-form narrative text. Long-form body typography strictly adheres to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or off-white (`#F1F5F9`) in dark mode, and deep slate (`#0F172A`) in light mode.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances editorial conviction with analytical density.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines):** Delivers a crisp, modern, confident voice. Generous apertures keep heavy weights legible at glance speeds, while geometric curves echo the system's rounded UI geometry.
- **Inter (Body & Data):** Neutral, utilitarian, and legible at small scales. Used for body text, perspective narratives, and comparative metrics.
- **Montserrat (Badges & Metrics):** Provides structural presence for topic pills, bias ratios, metadata stamps, and radar score trackers. Always set with uppercase tracking for instant identification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for multi-column split streams, trend telemetry, and side-by-side editorial deconstructions.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout, 24px gutters, 32px safe margins. Supports 3-pane workflows: Topic Navigator (3 cols), Main Perspective Feed (6 cols), and Radar Analytics / Trend Deck (3 cols).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Collapses navigation to an icon rail; perspectives stack into dual comparative cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px margins. Perspective split cards stack into an interactive swipeable segment or tabbed duel-view.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing behaves rhythmically using a strict 4px/8px incremental scale. Element groupings inside cards use `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px), while distinct perspective clusters separate via `space-lg` (24px) or `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects heavy drop shadows in favor of **structural contrast, tonal separation, and translucent borders**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** `#0B0F19` (dark) / `#F8FAFC` (light). Zero elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Feed Cards):** `#0F172A` (dark) / `#FFFFFF` (light). Encased with a 1px solid stroke (`#1E293B` in dark mode, `#E2E8F0` in light mode).
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals, Flyout Radars, Popovers):** `#1E293B` (dark) / `#FFFFFF` (light) with a diffuse ambient outline: `0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 230, 0, 0.25)` or `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

### Chromatic Glows
Interactive nodes, active radar nodes, and primary toggles utilize a restrained, high-saturation color halo: `0 0 16px rgba(255, 230, 0, 0.35)`. Magenta triggers a targeted glow (`rgba(255, 0, 127, 0.35)`) when flagging narrative conflicts.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp, high-contrast typography with **hyper-rounded forms (20px to 24px)** and **pill-shaped controls**, creating an approachable, tactile instrument.

- **Primary Cards & Perspective Split Units:** Defined with `rounded-xl` (24px corner radius) to soften complex data dense layouts.
- **Topic Pills & Action Chips:** Full pill shape (`9999px`) to maintain tactile distinction against square grid lines.
- **Circular Visual Anchors:** Radar source nodes, journalist avatars, and sentiment telemetry badges are set as perfect circles (`border-radius: 50%`) filled with saturated solid primary or secondary tones.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action Button:** Electric Yellow (`#FFE600`) background, deep obsidian (`#0B0F19`) text, bold Montserrat uppercase styling, full pill radius (`rounded-full`). On hover, scales subtly (`transform: scale(1.02)`) with a yellow chromatic halo.
- **Secondary Outlined Button:** Transparent fill, 2px border in `#334155` (or `#E2E8F0` in light mode), white label text, rounded pill radius.
- **Theme Switcher:** Dual-pill slider with dark obsidian and light cream icons, featuring an animated spring transition between modes.

### Topic Pills & Radar Badges
- Pill-shaped badges (`padding: 6px 14px`) with uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- Color mappings:
  - Consensus: Mint Cyan (`#00D09C`) tint with `#00D09C` border.
  - Conflict/Divergence: Vibrant Magenta (`#FF007F`) text and subtle stroke.
  - Developing Radar: Vivid Royal Blue (`#0066FF`) or Vibrant Orange (`#FF6B00`).

### Perspective Split Cards
- Central analytical building block. A single parent card (`rounded-xl`, 24px) split into bilateral perspective channels.
- Left column showcases Source A framing; right column showcases Source B framing. The center features a vertical Media Comparison Bar highlighting editorial bias, keyword disparity, and sentiment deltas.

### Media Comparison Bars & Sentiment Gauges
- Horizontal segmented progress bars (height: 8px, `rounded-full`) utilizing solid stops: Orange / Magenta (Left/Opposing), Mint Cyan (Centrist/Neutral), Royal Blue (Right/Alternative).

### Multi-Line Trend Charts
- Borderless SVG visualizations set against surface slate. Lines leverage thick 3px strokes in `#FFE600`, `#FF007F`, and `#00D09C`. Gridlines are strictly low-opacity `#334155` dashed lines. Points of maximum divergence feature circular beacon dots with ambient glow.

### Circular Icon Containers
- Sized at 32px, 40px, or 48px. Pure circular shapes (`rounded-full`) filled with high-voltage solid tints (Electric Yellow, Mint Cyan, Vibrant Magenta, Royal Blue) framing bold high-contrast iconography.
